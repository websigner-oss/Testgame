Suggested test checklist

1) Start a new run, move/attack, pick up gold/potions.
2) Gain XP and confirm the Level Up modal appears and applies upgrades.
3) Stand on stairs (>) and press Enter to open the Shop, then continue to the next floor.
4) Confirm auto-save: refresh and press Continue.
5) Die (or win) and submit a score to the local leaderboard.
